Privacy Policy — AhorraMapp

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the AhorraMapp app (the "app") collects, stores, uses and protects the information of the people who use it.

The app is a personal tool for recording product prices, stores and locations. Its design is based on one fundamental principle: all the data you enter is stored only on your own device and is never sent to any server owned by the developer. There is no user account, no own server and no synchronization between devices: no one else can see your data.

By using the app you accept the terms of this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app or uninstall it.

2. Data controller and contact

The data controller for the data derived from the use of this app is the developer of AhorraMapp.

For any privacy request, please contact us at:

  • Email: r.johan.developer@gmail.com

3. What data is collected and stored

The app works 100 % without connecting to any server of the developer. All the information you enter is stored in the internal, private storage of the app on your device, and photos are stored as local files in that same space. No data is sent to the developer or to third parties for collection purposes.

4. Why your data is not shared with other users

AhorraMapp has no servers of its own. There is no central place where user data is stored or processed, which makes it technically impossible for anyone else to see your information or for your data to be mixed with other users' data.

The app does not include:

  • User accounts, registration or sign-in.
  • Synchronization between devices.
  • Social features, leaderboards or public sharing of content.
  • Cloud storage managed by the developer.

Each device keeps its own independent local information. If you uninstall the app, that data disappears with you and no copies remain on any server.

5. External services and minimal data leaving your device

The app uses the internet as little as possible, only for features that require it. The internet uses that send data from your device are described below, with the exact data that leaves your device in each case and its purpose.

5.1 The map

To show you the map, the app downloads the images of the geographic area visible on your screen from an external map provider (CARTO). The data that leaves your device is the map area you are viewing and your IP address (standard connection data for any web request). No data of yours is transmitted: not product names, prices, photos or any other information.

This exchange is necessary to display the map inside the app. CARTO, as the map provider, handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

5.2 Finding addresses

When you search for an address or place a point on the map, the app uses the address service built into your device (on Android devices, Google's service) to convert the address into a map point, or the point into an address.

The data that leaves your device at that moment is only the address you search for or the point you want to convert. The result is saved only on your device so the app keeps working offline; it is not stored on any server of the developer.

6. Permissions the app requests

The app requests the following permissions, always with your prior consent:

Permission What it is used for
Location Calculating distances to the stores you record, centering the map and saving a product's location. Only used with your permission and in the foreground.
Camera Taking product photos to store them locally along with their prices.
Photo gallery Choosing existing product photos. The chosen photos are stored only on your device.

Photos taken or selected are never uploaded to the internet: they are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

7. How your data is stored and protected

  • Everything stays on your phone: the information is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by the system itself.
  • No server of our own: there is no server of the developer that receives, processes or stores user data.
  • No transmissions: except for the internet uses in section 5 (map and addresses), your data never leaves your device.
  • Full local control: since the data is yours and lives on your device, you directly control when it is created, changed or deleted.

8. Data retention

Data is kept on your device for as long as you decide: while you keep the app installed and do not delete it.

  • You can delete individual records at any time using the options the app offers.
  • When you uninstall the app (or clear its data from the system settings), all local information is permanently deleted, with no copy remaining on any server.
  • The only information that remains outside your control is what the map provider receives while you use the map (section 5.1), governed by that provider's rules.

9. Your rights

Since the app does not collect your data on its own servers, control is in your hands:

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit all your records directly within the app.
  • Complete deletion: the easiest way to exercise your right of erasure is to uninstall the app or clear its data from your device settings. Doing so permanently deletes all local information.
  • Revoking permissions: you can deny or revoke location, camera or gallery permissions from the system settings at any time.
  • Portability: the app does not prevent you from exporting or using your information; as it is stored locally, it always remains under your control.

If you live in a region with data protection regulations (for example, the GDPR in the European Union or equivalent laws), the rights described above are understood to be exercised in accordance with those rules. For any claim or request, contact us through the channels in section 2.

10. Sale, transfer or use for advertising

AhorraMapp does not sell, rent or transfer your personal data to third parties. There is no centralized data collection and no third-party advertising services are used inside the app.

The only exception to data transmission is what is strictly necessary for the map to work as described in section 5 (the images of the area you view and address searches), which happens directly between your device and the relevant provider, without the developer acting as an intermediary.

11. Children's privacy

The app is aimed at the general public and has no age requirement for everyday use. However, since no personal data is collected on servers, the developer does not process children's data.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has misused the app or has questions about this policy, they can contact us through the channels in section 2. Remember that uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all the information stored on the device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical or legal changes. Any change will be published on this page, and the "last updated" date at the top will be updated accordingly.

Because the app does not collect personal data on servers, changes to this policy do not require you to re-consent for previously stored data: your data always remains on your device, under your control.
